About The Institution

Established with a vision to impart accessible and high-grade education to the youth of Malkapur and surrounding rural areas, Shriman G. R. Warange Junior College stands as a respected seat of higher secondary education in Buldhana district.

The institution offers full-time Class XI and Class XII coursework recognized by the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education, Amravati Divisional Board. Our campus provides a structured academic environment supported by experienced faculty members, modern laboratories, and a well-stocked library.

Campus Life & Discipline: Daily morning prayer assembly with students and faculty members at Shriman G. R. Warange Junior College, Malkapur.

Academic Streams & Subject Combinations

The college offers two-year Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) programs in Science, Commerce, and Arts faculties as per Maharashtra State Board standards.

Stream / Faculty Compulsory Subjects Optional & Elective Subjects
Science Class XI & XII English, Physics, Chemistry Mathematics & Statistics, Biology, Information Technology / Marathi
Commerce Class XI & XII English, Book-Keeping & Accountancy, Economics Organization of Commerce & Management (OCM), Secretarial Practice (SP) / Mathematics, Marathi
Arts Class XI & XII English, Marathi History, Geography, Political Science, Economics, Sociology

Campus Infrastructure & Facilities

Our campus is equipped with essential educational amenities to ensure comprehensive academic development:

  • Science Laboratories: Well-maintained Physics, Chemistry, and Biology practical laboratories compliant with State Board norms.
  • Computer Center: Computer facility for Information Technology (IT) practical sessions and digital literacy.
  • Central Library: Comprehensive collection of prescribed textbooks, reference literature, and daily newspapers.
  • Sports & Athletics: Outdoor playground supporting volleyball, kabaddi, kho-kho, and annual sports events.
